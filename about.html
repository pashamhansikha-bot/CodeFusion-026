From an Andhra kitchen to your car window.

Ruchulu Junction started as a single tiffin stall on the Vijayawada–Guntur highway, run by a family that believed spice, tang, and generous portions shouldn't wait for a sit-down table. Today the recipes are the same — gongura picked and pounded fresh, dum biryani sealed and slow-cooked, chilli ground the Guntur way — just faster to your window.
